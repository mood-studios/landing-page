Before your visit

Studio guidelines

To ensure a pleasant experience for all clients, we kindly ask that you review and follow our studio guidelines. Your cooperation helps us maintain a safe and beautiful space for everyone.

Equipment care & damage
  • Please handle all studio equipment, furniture, props, and accessories with care.
  • Any damage to equipment, furniture, props, lighting, or cameras due to misuse, negligence, or accidents will be charged accordingly.
  • Fees will be charged if backdrops are damaged, torn, stained, watered, written on, or improperly handled.
  • Clients are not allowed to adjust or move lighting equipment unless assisted by studio staff.
Companions & additional pax
  • Only one (1) companion is allowed per session.
  • If a companion joins the shoot or appears in photos, additional pax fees will apply.
  • Children must be supervised at all times by an adult companion.
Pet policy
  • We love furry friends! Pets are always welcome in our studio, and we’re happy to include them in your session while keeping the space safe and comfortable for everyone.
  • Pets must be clean, well-groomed, and diapered.
  • Owners are kindly asked to keep their pets supervised at all times.
  • Please bring treats, toys, or anything that helps keep your pet calm and happy during the shoot.
  • Any accidents, stains, or damage caused by pets may be subject to corresponding cleaning or damage fees.
Session time & overtime
  • Sessions start and end strictly at the scheduled time.
  • Please arrive on time; late arrivals may result in reduced shooting time.
  • Staying inside the studio for more than 10 minutes after the session ends is not allowed.
  • Overtime fees will apply if you exceed your booked time.
Cleanliness & safety
  • No smoking, vaping, or alcohol inside the studio.
  • Please keep personal belongings within designated areas.
  • The studio is not responsible for lost or unattended items.
